# Chord Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

Chord is a Safari extension that toggles Picture in Picture and copies the current tab's address to the clipboard.

**Chord does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- **No network access.** Chord does not connect to the internet and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- **Page access.** To find the video on a page, Chord's content script runs on the websites you allow in Safari. It only reads which `<video>` elements exist and their playback state. Nothing from the page is saved or sent anywhere.
- **Tab address.** When you press the copy shortcut, Chord reads the current tab's address and writes it to your Mac's clipboard. It is not stored or sent anywhere.
- **Settings.** The only stored setting is whether to show the "Address copied" toast. It stays on your Mac.

You control which websites Chord can access in Safari Settings → Extensions → Chord.
